    Terms, Conditions & Pricing Policy

    Estimated Pricing

    The prices shown in this quote tool are estimates based on the information you provide. Your final confirmed price will be issued by Akita Air Conditioning following a review of your submission. Prices are inclusive of supply, standard installation, labour, and consumables.

    Accuracy of Information

    It is your responsibility to provide accurate information when completing this form. If the information supplied — including room size, pipe run distance, floor level, property type, or access details — differs from actual site conditions, Akita Air Conditioning reserves the right to amend the quoted price before or on the day of installation. Additional charges will apply where the actual work required exceeds what was described.

    Additional Charges May Apply For

    Pipe runs longer than stated • Electrical work where no socket is present • Scaffolding or access equipment • Non-standard installation requirements discovered on site • Any other variation from the information provided in this form.

    Booking & Cancellation

    Selecting a date through this tool is a booking request only and does not constitute a confirmed appointment until you receive written confirmation from Akita Air Conditioning. Cancellations must be made at least 48 hours before the scheduled installation date.

    Warranties

    All installations include a 12-month Akita workmanship warranty. Manufacturer warranties of up to 5 years are available subject to annual service being maintained. Warranty terms are provided at the time of installation.

    Privacy

    Your personal details will be used solely to process your quote and installation booking. We will not share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
